Three Tailored Portals

ImpactHub provides strictly separated dashboards and authentication flows designed specifically for students, volunteers, and platform administrators.

Student Portal

For undergraduates seeking guidance. Request one-on-one sessions with your assigned mentor, track academic progress, and access curated development resources.

  • Personalized mentor guidance notes
  • Schedule & manage guidance sessions
  • Track learning resources & milestones
Student Login → Register as Student

Volunteer Portal

For caring mentors, alumni, and industry advisors. Review incoming student guidance requests, schedule sessions, document developmental feedback, and set availability.

  • Review & approve student requests
  • Document confidential guidance notes
  • Manage weekly availability schedules
Volunteer Login → Apply as Volunteer

Admin Portal

For program directors and guidance coordinators. Oversee mentor pairings, approve volunteer applications, publish development activities, and monitor platform analytics.

  • Assign & reassign student mentors
  • Review volunteer credentials & approval
  • Live analytics & demonstration reset
Admin Console → Review Credentials

How ImpactHub Works

A structured, human-centric approach ensuring every student receives tailored academic and career empowerment.

1

Connect & Pair

Students register with their academic discipline, year, and focus areas. Administrators review profiles and pair students with vetted volunteer mentors based on mutual expertise.

2

Request & Guide

Students request dedicated guidance sessions on specific topics â€” from capstone architecture to interview preparation. Mentors schedule sessions and provide constructive feedback notes.

3

Track & Flourish

Participants track milestone progress through curated learning resources, campus workshops, and collaborative activities, maintaining longitudinal growth across the academic year.
